Season Highlights

🏆 Championship

F-Division Champions

The Mustangs captured the F-Division title, continuing to assert their dominance in divisional play and reaffirming Marlboro as one of the premier wrestling programs in the Shore Conference.

📜 History

First Division Title Since 1971

For the first time in over 50 years, Marlboro claimed a division championship — a monumental moment for the program and its community.

🥊 State Sectionals

Highest-Ever Sectional Seed: 4th

Marlboro earned the highest seed in program history heading into state sectionals — then made even more history by winning their first-ever state sectional match.

🏅 Districts

4th Place at District Championships

The team finished 4th at the district championships and sent 5 wrestlers to the NJ Region Championships — the most qualifiers in recent memory.

⚡ Record

22 Wins: New Season Record

The team shattered the previous single-season win record of 19 (set in 2020) with an incredible 22-win campaign, the best in program history.

🎯 Milestone

100th Team Win for Coach Frankel

Head Coach Charlie Frankel recorded his 100th team victory this season — a testament to years of dedication, discipline, and championship-level leadership.

Coach Charlie Frankel

Recognized for his decades of leadership, his mentorship of countless student-athletes, and his role in transforming Marlboro wrestling into a program of distinction, Coach Frankel joins a select group of legends whose contributions to Marlboro athletics will never be forgotten.
